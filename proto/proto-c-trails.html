Prototype note

Movement, drawn as light.

Twelve ribbons trace player runs through 3D space — curving, accelerating, cutting back. Each is a tube built along a procedural curve, coloured with your brand gradient and lit from within. It's abstract rather than literal: the feeling of movement and intelligence, without a stock photo in sight.

Interaction: the ribbon field parallaxes with your cursor and accelerates as you scroll — the faster you move, the faster the runs.
