Prototype note

A real match ball, built from maths.

This is a genuine truncated icosahedron — 12 pentagons and 20 hexagons, the same solid every modern football is stitched from. The panel layout is computed as a spherical Voronoi diagram over 32 seed points, so the seams are geometrically correct rather than drawn by hand. Seams render as glowing tubes, neural arcs fire across the surface, and orbit rings trace movement around it. No images, no model files — all of it generated in code from your four brand colours.

Interaction: the sphere responds to your cursor — move the mouse across the hero and the ball tilts toward it. Scroll speed also feeds the rotation. Drag to spin it directly.
